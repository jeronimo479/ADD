**Module Title: Shadows of Deeping Cave**  
**System**: AD&D 2nd Edition  
**Levels**: 2–4 (recommended for 4–6 characters; reconnaissance focus allows stealth, negotiation, or hit-and-run)  
**Setting**: Forgotten Realms, starting in Triboar (Dessarin Vale), leading into Kryptgarden Forest and Deeping Cave (Old Gnawbone’s former lair).  
**Tone**: Atmospheric horror/exploration with undead, aberrations, and moral choices. Emphasis on scouting and survival over conquest.  
**Time**: Immediately after *The Lost Tomb of the Dwarven Prince* (or concurrent with its hooks).  

### Adventure Background
The Alhoon **Val’Xootiz** (an illithilich) and its retinue of warlocks and thralls emerged from the Underdark months ago. They seized Deeping Cave while Old Gnawbone (Claugiyliamatar) was absent or weakened. The Alhoon’s necrotic/illithid magic corrupts the forest, causing game to flee, goblins to starve and raid, undead to rise, and sylvan creatures to flee or turn hostile.  

The tomb enchantment on Prince Torarinn awoke due to this evil taint. Lord Protector in Triboar (or a patron such as Garmund of Kelemvor, Urgala Meltimer, or a worried merchant) hires the PCs for a **reconnaissance mission**: infiltrate the area, determine the nature/strength of the threat, number and type of minions, and apparent goals — then return alive with the intelligence. Reward: 500–800 gp total (plus expenses, magic items, and reputation). Failure risks the Vale falling under shadow.  

**Alhoon’s Goals** (for DM only): Val’Xootiz seeks to create a hidden sanctum for illithiliches, experiment with draconic/undead hybrids, and build a thrall army using the Green Dragon Mask fragments or local magic. It scries on the Vale and plots expansion.

- Strange lights and howls from the forest.  
- Starving goblins raiding.  
- Undead sightings.  
- Rumors of a “mind-eater” or lich in Deeping Cave.

**Preparation**: Stock up on holy water, rations, antitoxins, ropes, and stealth gear. A local ranger or druid NPC can give +2 on forest navigation if hired (50 gp).  

### Part 2: Journey to Kryptgarden (Wilderness Encounters)
Travel time: 1–2 days. Use random encounters from the tomb module + these:  

**Hidden Treasure Opportunity**: In the foothills, a ruined Besilmer waystation (DC 13 Intelligence (History) or Stonecunning to recognize).  
- **Trap**: Collapsing ceiling (DC 14 Dex save or 2d6 damage; Search DC 15).  
- **Encounter**: 4 skeletons (HD 1, HP 6, 8, 5, 7; AC 7; THAC0 19; 1 attack 1d6) guarding a desecrated altar.  
- **Treasure**: 120 gp in ancient coins, *potion of healing* (2 doses), dwarven helm (+1 AC vs. blunt, non-magical). Hidden compartment (DC 16 Find Secret Doors): *scroll of protection from undead*.  

**Forest Encounters** (roll d6 every 4 hours daylight / 2 hours night):  
1–2: 6 starving goblins (HD 1-1, HP avg 4; AC 6; short sword or spear 1d6; starving: -1 to hit/damage). Can negotiate (food buys info: “mind-eater in cave”).  
3: Giant wolf spiders (as tomb) or 1 owlbear (wounded).  
4: Undead patrol: 2 zombies + 1 ghoul (scout for Alhoon).  
5–6: Harmless (sylvan signs of corruption: dead dryads, twisted trees).  

**Deeping Cave Approach**: A gloomy ravine at the forest-mountain edge, overgrown with vines and phosphorescent fungi. Entrance hidden by hanging moss (DC 12 Spot). Guards: 2 goblin scouts + 1 thrall (see below).

#### Keyed Areas (Fully Detailed)

**Area 1: Ravine Entrance** (Ambush)  
Mossy gully. 4 goblins (HD 1-1, HP 5,4,6,3; AC 6; short sword 1d6) + 1 goblin boss (HD 3, HP 18; AC 5; morningstar 1d8+1).  
**Tactics**: Ambush from above (surprise if PCs not stealthy). Flee if half dead, revealing “mind-flayer master.”  
**Treasure**: 25 gp, crude map to entrance.  

**Area 2: Main Cavern – Fungal Grove** (Exploration/Combat)  
Giant toadstools, statues draped in moss (DC 14 Spot hidden: one statue has gem eyes worth 200 gp total, trapped with contact poison—Type F, DC 16 save or paralyzed 2d6 hours).  
- **Minions**: 6 thralls (human commoners turned zombies: HD 2, HP 12 each; AC 8; slam 1d8; slow). Patrolling.  
- **Encounter**: If loud, 1d4 stirges (HD 1+1, HP 5–8; AC 8; blood drain) from ceiling.  
**Hidden Room** (DC 16 secret door behind statue): Alhoon’s discarded experiments—2 preserved illithid tadpoles in jars (alchemical value 300 gp to wizard).  

**Area 3: Guard Post** (Side Chamber)  
2 warlocks (human 3rd-level mages: HD 3, HP 14,16; AC 6 (bracers); spells: *magic missile* x2, *shield*, *sleep*; dagger 1d4). + 3 skeletons.  
**Trap**: Glyph of warding on door (explosion 2d8 fire, DC 14 save half; Search DC 17).  
**Treasure**: Spellbook (low-level), 80 gp, potions of *invisibility* (1) and *healing* (2).  

**Area 4: Scrying Alcove** (Core Intel)  
Glowing crystal balls (3; one shows Triboar, one tomb area, one Underdark route). Val’Xootiz here (see stats).  
**Alhoon Val’Xootiz** (advanced alhoon): HD 8+4, HP 55; AC 5; MV 12; THAC0 11; 4 tentacle attacks 1d4 + mind flayer extract brain (save vs. death or die); spells as 9th-level wizard (necromancy/illusion focus: *finger of death*, *hold person*, *improved invisibility*, etc.); psionics; undead immunities + magic resistance 25%. Goal: Build thrall army, corrupt forest as base. Strength: Powerful but not ancient dragon-level; vulnerable to sunlight/turn undead (weaker than true lich). Minions: ~20–30 total (goblins 12, thralls 10, warlocks 4, undead 6+).  
**Tactics**: Scry, then dominate or flee via secret tunnel if threatened. Parley possible if PCs stealthy/diplomatic.  

**Area 5: Dragon’s Bed / Hoard Chamber**  
Corrupted coin bed (reduced hoard). Hanging moss conceals pit trap to lower cave (20 ft, spikes 2d6).  
**Treasure**: 800 gp coins/gems, *Green Dragon Mask fragment* (plot hook, minor powers + curse), ancient statues (art 1,200 gp if recovered), *ring of protection +1*. Hidden: Dragon’s old journal (reveals Alhoon weakness).  

**Area 6–8: Side Tunnels / Underdark Entrance** (Optional Deeper)  
More thralls, a captive dryad (info on corruption), and escape route. Trap: Illithid slime pool (save vs. poison or hallucinate 1d4 hours).  

**Additional Traps**:  
- **Fungal Hallucination Spores** (DC 15 save or confused 1 turn).  
- **Collapsing Ravine Wall** on exit (pursuit hazard).

- Warlocks: Mage stats.  
- Undead: Skeletons/ghouls.
