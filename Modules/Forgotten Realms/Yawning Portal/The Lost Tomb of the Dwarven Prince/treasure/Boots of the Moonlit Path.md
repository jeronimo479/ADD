# Boots of the Moonlit Path 

## Description 

Soft gray leather boots that never accumulate mud. 

## Powers 

* Three times per night, the wearer may step into one shadow and emerge from
another shadow within 60 feet. 

* Leave no tracks unless desired. 

* Double movement rate when traveling beneath moonlight. 

## Limitation 

The shadow-step ability functions only during darkness, underground, or under
overcast skies.
