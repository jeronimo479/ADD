# Green Dragon Mask

## Description

Crafted from the scales and skull fragments of an ancient green dragon, this 
emerald mask radiates powerful enchantment and constantly exhales faint traces 
of toxic vapors. Those who wear it gain a measure of a green dragon's cunning, 
resilience, and deceitful nature.

## Powers

### Breath of the Green Wyrm

Once per day, the wearer may unleash a cone of poisonous gas 30 feet long.

All creatures within the area of effect must make a **Saving Throw vs. Breath 
Weapon**.

- Success: Half damage.
- Failure: 6d6 points of poison damage.

Creatures immune to poison are unaffected.

### Dragon-Blessed Constitution

While worn, the mask grants:

- +4 bonus on all **Saving Throws vs. Poison**
- Immunity to normal animal and insect venoms
- Against magical poisons, a failed saving throw may be immediately rerolled 
  once per exposure

### Tongue of the Deceiver

The wearer gains an uncanny gift for manipulation.

- +2 reaction adjustment when bargaining, threatening, deceiving, or negotiating
- +20% bonus to relevant Nonweapon Proficiencies such as **Etiquette**, 
  **Fast Talking**, **Persuasion**, or similar proficiencies at the DM's 
  discretion

### Draconic Insight

Once per day, the wearer may invoke the cunning of the dragon spirit bound 
within the mask.

The wearer gains a **+4 bonus to a single Intelligence- or Charisma-based 
proficiency check**.

## Curse: The Draconic Seed

The mask slowly influences its wearer toward the mindset of a green dragon.

After each month of continuous use, the wearer must make a **Saving Throw vs. 
Spell**.

Failure causes one of the following effects:

1. Growing suspicion of companions
2. Obsession with wealth, secrets, and influence
3. Reluctance to share information or treasures
4. Desire to dominate rather than cooperate

These effects are cumulative.

After three failed saving throws, the wearer views even trusted allies as 
potential rivals and may refuse to surrender the mask willingly.

A *Remove Curse* spell suppresses these influences for one week but does not 
destroy them. Only permanent separation from the mask or powerful magical 
intervention can completely eliminate its effects.
