# The Ring of the Last Witness 

## Description 

A silver ring set with a milky opal. Within the gem, tiny ghostly scenes
occasionally appear. 

## Powers 

* The wearer can, once per day, touch an object and witness the last significant
event that occurred within 20 feet of it during the past week. 

* The vision lasts 1d4 rounds and appears as translucent images only visible to
the wearer. 

* Grants +2 on proficiency checks involving investigation, tracking, or
detecting deception. 

## Curse/Burden 

Each use has a 10% chance of forcing the wearer to relive the most traumatic
event witnessed by the ring's former owners, causing fear for 1d6 rounds.
