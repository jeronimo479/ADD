# Green Dragon Mask — Artifact of Viridian Dominion

Wondrous Item (Artifact), Requires Attunement

Forged from overlapping scales harvested from an ancient green wyrm, the Green
Dragon Mask is a sinister relic steeped in toxic magic. Its surface gleams with
an oily emerald sheen, and faint wisps of viridian vapor curl from the nostrils
when the wearer breathes.

## Origin
The mask was discovered on an obsidian pedestal deep within the Gnawbone's
lair’s inner sanctum, guarded by thorn covered constructs and pools of acidic
mist.

## Properties
- Corrosive Breath Channeling: Once per short rest, you can unleash a 30ft cone
of viridian poison.
- Poisonbound Flesh: Resistance to poison damage and advantage on saving throws
against poison.
- Voice of Verdant Deceit: Bonuses to Deception and Persuasion checks when
lying, threatening, or negotiating.
- Dragon’s Cunning Insight: Once per long rest, roll an extra die on an 
Intelligence or Charisma check.

## Cursed Aspect - The Draconic Seed
The wearer experiences growing fondness for manipulation, distrust of allies,
and a desire to cultivate power.

## Appearance
A stylized draconic skull of emerald scales with faint green fumes escaping from
nostrils and eye slits.
