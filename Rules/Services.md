# AD&D 2nd Edition House Rules  
**Currency System & Town Services**

## Coin Exchange Rates  
(Exactly as listed in the Player’s Handbook, page 66)

| Coin Type       | Copper Pieces (CP) | Silver Pieces (SP) | Electrum Pieces (EP) | Gold Pieces (GP) | Platinum Pieces (PP) |
|-----------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------------------|------------------|----------------------|
| 1 Copper (CP)   | 1                  | 1/10               | 1/50                 | 1/100            | 1/500                |
| 1 Silver (SP)   | 10                 | 1                  | 1/5                  | 1/10             | 1/50                 |
| 1 Electrum (EP) | 50                 | 5                  | 1                    | 1/2              | 1/10                 |
| 1 Gold (GP)     | 100                | 10                 | 2                    | 1                | 1/5                  |
| 1 Platinum (PP) | 500                | 50                 | 10                   | 5                | 1                    |

### Gear Repair
All repairs are charged as a percentage of the item's original (new) cost.

| Repair Type      | Cost                     |
|------------------|--------------------------|
| Minor Repair     | 10% of original cost     |
| Major Repair     | 25% of original cost     |
