# AD&D Poison Quick Reference Sheet

**Core Rules (DMG pp. 78–79, Table 51)**  
**Saving Throw**: vs. Poison at onset time (Constitution bonus applies).  
**Fail**: Full effect (damage or death drops to 0 HP). **Success**: Reduced effect or none.  
Damage is immediate & healable; death is permanent unless cured timely.  
**Modifiers**: Victim Size vs. Attacker  
`  4x larger:+4  2x larger:+2  ½ as large:-2  ¼ as large:-4  
`  +4 vs. small creature poison on large targets;  
`  - elves / dwarves get +1/+½.  
**Monster Venoms**: Onset usually 1-6 rounds (1 round for 5+ HD creatures); match to table type.

## Delivery Methods
- **Injected (Injury)**: Via weapon, bite, or sting. 1 dose coats 1 melee weapon or 4 arrows/bolts. Dries after 2 hits or 1 turn. 5% self-poison risk per round (halved for assassins; Dexterity modifiers apply).  
- **Ingested**: Via food or drink. Half effect if injected instead.  
- **Contact**: Via skin touch (e.g., traps, coated objects/coins). Full effect even if ingested/injected. Potency fades: full (1 month), half (6 months), quarter (1 year), tenth (18+ months). 1 dose coats 1 object or 12 small items.  
- **Aerosol/Gas (Inhaled)**: Thrown vial bursts into 10x10x10 ft cloud (expands 1-2 rounds, lasts 1 turn). Uses Ingested A-D effects; x10 cost. Saving throw = hold breath (no effect). Type E variant: sleep 1-6 turns (onset 1-3 rounds).  

**Monster Venom Harvesting (Dragon #81)**: 50% success chance. Yields: 1 dose (small creature), 1d3 doses (medium), 1d6 doses (large). Unprocessed lasts 1 week; refine to table types.

## Cures & Antidotes
- **Neutralize Poison (spell)**: Fully negates poison (does not restore hit points). Cast before onset completes.  
- **Slow Poison (spell)**: Delays lethal effects (victim loses only 1 hit point per turn); buys time for full cure.  
- **Antidote**: Specific to poison type & method (e.g., D Ingested counters A-D Ingested). Take before onset or up to 3 hours prior to exposure. Cost ~ poison price (alchemists/assassin guilds). Monster-specific: 1,000 gp per HD (halved with venom sample).  
- **Holy Water (drunk)**: Delays onset 2d4+1 turns; revives victim if given within 1 turn of "death" (1 use per poisoning).  
- **Healing Proficiency (PHB Ch. 5)**: +2 bonus to saving throw vs. poison. **Injected**: Tend immediately for full 5 rounds (healer & victim sacrifice all actions). **Ingested/Contact**: Requires + Herbalism proficiency (induce vomiting/poultice). 1 attempt per poisoning.  

**Detection**: Taste/smell checks (elves/dwarves better); *detect poison* spell.
