## Classes

### Warrior

The warrior group encompasses the character classes whose primary role is
combat: fighters, paladins, and rangers.

Warriors can wear any type of armor and use the widest selection of weapons.
They gain 1d10 hit points per level from levels 1 through 9. After 9th level,
warriors gain a fixed 3 hit points per level and no longer receive additional
Constitution hit point bonuses.

Warriors gain multiple attacks as they advance in level.

| Warrior Level | Attacks Per Round |
|---------------|-------------------|
| 1-6           | 1/round           |
| 7-12          | 3/2 rounds        |
| 13+           | 2/round           |

#### Fighter
A fighter with a strength score greater than 15 receives a 10% experience bonus.

**Weapon Specialization**
: Weapon specialization is available only to single-class fighters. By devoting
additional weapon proficiency slots to a specific weapon, a fighter gains
greater accuracy, increased damage, and additional attacks with that weapon.

To specialize, a fighter must first be proficient with the weapon and then spend
one additional weapon proficiency slot.

A fighter specialized in a melee weapon gains +1 to attack and +2 to damage
rolls. This costs 1 extra proficiency.

A fighter specialized with a bow gains a point-blank range (6-30') that adds +2
to attack rolls. This costs 2 extra proficiency slots.

A fighter specialized in crossbows gain a point-blank range (6-60') that adds +2
to attack rolls. This costs 1 extra proficiency slot.

If the bow or crossbow is ready at the start of a round, they may fire before
initiative rolls.

#### Paladin

A paladin is a heroic human warrior dedicated to the cause of law and good.

| Ability Requirement | Minimum Score |
|---------------------|---------------|
| Strength            | 12            |
| Constitution        | 9             |
| Wisdom              | 13            |
| Charisma            | 17            |

A paladin with Strength and Charisma scores of 16 or greater gains a 10%
bonus to earned experience points.

Paladins must always remain Lawful Good. A paladin who knowingly commits an
evil act immediately and irrevocably loses paladin status and all special
abilities. A paladin who changes alignment loses paladin abilities until
atonement is made.

A paladin receives the following benefits:

**Detect Evil**: Concentrate to detect evil intent up to 60 feet away.  
**Divine Saving Throws**: +2 bonus to all saving throws.  
**Disease Immunity**: Immune to all forms of disease.  
**Lay on Hands**: Heal 2 hit points per experience level per day.  
**Cure Disease**: Cure disease once per week for every 5 levels.  
**Protection from Evil**: Constant 10-foot radius aura. A paladin's aura of
protection causes evil creatures within 10 feet to suffer a -1 penalty to
attack rolls.  
**Turn Undead**: Functions as a cleric two levels lower.  
**Holy Sword**: Projects a dispel magic effect when wielded.  

A paladin can call a special warhorse upon reaching 4th level. The mount is an
exceptional and loyal creature that serves only the paladin.

A paladin can cast priest spells beginning at 9th level. Paladin spells are
drawn from the spheres of combat, divination, healing, and protection.

Paladins may never possess more than 10 magical items. No more than one suit
of magical armor, one magical shield, four magical weapons, and four other
magical items may be owned at one time.

A paladin never retains wealth beyond what is necessary to maintain a modest
lifestyle, support followers, maintain a stronghold, and continue good works.
Excess wealth must be donated to lawful good causes.

A paladin attracts loyal followers and men-at-arms upon reaching 9th level and
establishing a stronghold.

A paladin may employ only lawful good henchmen and followers. Associates of
other good alignments are tolerated only when they actively pursue good causes.

#### Ranger

Rangers can be human, elf, or half-elf.

| Ability Requirement | Minimum Score |
|---------------------|---------------|
| Strength            | 13            |
| Dexterity           | 13            |
| Constitution        | 14            |
| Wisdom              | 14            |

A ranger with Strength, Dexterity, and Wisdom scores of 16 or greater gains a
10% bonus to earned experience points.

Rangers may be Lawful Good, Neutral Good, Chaotic Good, Lawful Neutral, True
Neutral, or Chaotic Neutral.

Rangers can use any weapon and wear any armor.

The ranger ignores normal two-weapon penalties while wearing studded leather
armor or lighter.

Rangers are skilled woodsmen and automatically possess the Tracking ability
without spending proficiency slots. Their tracking ability improves as they
gain levels. When tracking, a ranger gains a +1 per 3 levels attained.

Rangers gain a +4 attack bonus against a chosen species of giant-class enemy.
Typical giant-class enemies include: Giants, Ogres, Trolls, Ettins, Titans

Rangers are skilled with animals and can attempt to calm, befriend, or train
domestic and wild animals through careful handling and observation.

A ranger can identify general traits and temperament in domestic animals and
may influence an animal's reaction with successful interaction.

Rangers can cast priest spells beginning at 8th level. Ranger spells are drawn
from the spheres of animal and plant.

A wizard's Intelligence determines the highest spell level that can be learned,
the chance to learn spells, and the maximum number of spells that can be known
per spell level.

Wizards begin play with a spellbook containing a small number of 1st-level
spells. Additional spells are acquired through research, instruction, copying,
or adventuring.

Arcane magic is divided into schools of magic:

- Abjuration
- Alteration
- Conjuration/Summoning
- Divination
- Enchantment/Charm
- Illusion/Phantasm
- Invocation/Evocation
- Necromancy

A mage studies all schools of magic. A specialist wizard focuses on a single
school of magic, gaining advantages within that school while giving up access
to one or more opposing schools.

The nine schools of magic are: Abjuration, Alteration, Conjuration/Summoning,
Enchantment/Charm, Greater Divination, Illusion, Invocation/Evocation, Necromancy,
and Lesser Divination.

#### Specialist Wizard

A specialist wizard concentrates on a single school of magic.

A specialist gains the following benefits:

- One additional spell slot per spell level, provided the additional spell is
  from the specialist's chosen school.
- A +15% bonus when learning spells from the specialist school.
- A -15% penalty when learning spells from schools outside the specialty.
- A +1 bonus to saving throws against spells from the specialist school.
- Opponents suffer a -1 penalty on saving throws against the specialist's
  spells from the specialty school.
- When researching new spells, spells from the specialist school are treated
  as one level lower in difficulty.

A specialist may learn and cast spells only from schools available to the
specialty. Opposing schools are prohibited.

Specialists cannot be multiclass characters except for gnome illusionists.

#### Illusionist

An illusionist is a specialist wizard devoted to the schools of illusion and
phantasm. Only gnomes may be multiclass illusionists.

| Ability Requirement | Minimum Score |
|---------------------|---------------|
| Dexterity           | 16            |
| Intelligence        | 15            |

A gnome illusionist with an Intelligence score of 16 or greater gains a 10%
bonus to earned experience points.

In addition to the normal specialist wizard benefits, illusionists gain the
following abilities:

- Receive a +1 bonus on saving throws against illusion spells.
- Opponents suffer a -1 penalty on saving throws against the illusionist's
  illusion spells.
- At 1st level, begin play knowing one additional illusion spell beyond the
  normal starting allotment, provided an additional illusion spell is
  available.
- Receive a +15% bonus when learning illusion spells.
- Receive a -15% penalty when learning spells from non-specialist schools.
- Researching illusion spells is easier than researching spells from other
  schools.

Illusionists may learn spells only from the following schools:

- Alteration
- Conjuration/Summoning
- Greater Divination
- Illusion/Phantasm

At 1st level, an illusionist automatically knows the spell *Color Spray*.

### Priest

The priest group consists of clerics and druids.

All priests possess the following abilities:

- Cast priest spells.
- Perform priestly duties on behalf of their faith.
- Act as representatives of their deity or philosophy.
- Gain magical powers through divine favor.

Priests are restricted in their choice of weapons and armor according to the
requirements of their faith.

Priest spells are divided into spheres. A priest can cast spells only from the
major and minor spheres granted by his deity or doctrine.

Thieves may wear leather armor, padded armor, elven chain, and magical armor

**Pick Pockets**
: Pick pockets allows a thief to remove small items from a victim without being
noticed. Failure does not automatically alert the victim. A separate check
determines whether the attempt is detected.

**Open Locks**
: Open locks allows a thief to bypass padlocks, combination locks, puzzle locks,
and similar mechanisms using specialized tools.

**Find/Remove Traps**
: This skill allows a thief to locate and disable small mechanical traps,
primarily those associated with locks, treasure chests, doors, and similar
devices.

**Move Silently**
: Move silently measures the thief's ability to approach without being heard.
The thief must move carefully at approximately one-third normal movement speed.

**Hide in Shadows**
: Hide in shadows allows a thief to conceal himself in darkness or natural cover.
The ability requires suitable concealment and cannot normally be used while
being directly observed.

**Detect Noise**
: Detect noise allows a thief to hear faint sounds behind doors, around corners,
or through barriers. The thief must remain still and concentrate for one round.

**Climb Walls**
: Thieves can climb surfaces impossible for most characters. This ability allows
climbing sheer walls and similar surfaces without ropes or special equipment,
provided handholds are available.

**Read Languages**
: Read languages allows the thief to understand written material in unfamiliar
languages. The roll is made for each document. Success allows partial or complete
understanding of the text.

**Thieves' Cant**
: All thieves learn a secret language known as thieves' cant. Thieves' cant
consists of coded phrases, slang, gestures, and expressions used to exchange
information without attracting attention. The cant is suitable for discussing
jobs, fences, safe houses, contacts, rewards, and criminal activities.

**Use Scrolls**
: Beginning at 10th level, a thief can attempt to use wizard scrolls. The thief
reads the scroll and attempts to activate the contained spell despite lacking
formal magical training.

#### Bard

A bard can be human, elf, or half-elf.

| Ability Requirement | Minimum Score |
|---------------------|---------------|
| Dexterity           | 12            |
| Intelligence        | 13            |
| Charisma            | 15            |

A bard with Dexterity and Charisma scores of 16 or greater gains a 10% bonus
to earned experience points.

Bards may be of any alignment except lawful good. A bard must remain at least
partially neutral to successfully fulfill the role.

Bards can use any weapon and wear any armor up to, and including chain mail, but
cannot use shields.

All bards are proficient singers, musicians, storytellers, poets, and
entertainers. They begin play knowing how to play one musical instrument. Each
additional musical instrument requires an additional proficiency slot.

Bards learn arcane magic in much the same way as wizards. They maintain
spellbooks and cast wizard spells. However, they are dabblers rather than
dedicated wizards and advance more slowly in magical ability.

A bard's Intelligence determines the highest spell level that can be learned,
the chance to learn spells, and the maximum number of spells known per spell
level.

**Climb Walls**
: A bard can climb steep surfaces without tools or ropes.

**Detect Noise**
: A bard can hear faint sounds behind doors, around corners, or through
barriers, but must remain still and concentrate for one round.

**Pick Pockets**
: A bard can perform sleight-of-hand tricks, palm small objects, entertain a
crowd, or quietly remove items from another person.

**Read Languages**
: A bard can decipher unfamiliar writing and understand fragments of unknown
languages. Success indicates partial or complete understanding of the text.

**Bardic Lore**
: Bards accumulate knowledge through travel, study, observation, and
storytelling. The DM may allow a bard to recognize legends, historical references,
notable persons, magical traditions, heraldry, songs, customs, or local folklore
that other characters would not know.

**Inspire and Influence**
: A bard can perform before an audience using music, poetry, storytelling,
oratory, comedy, or other performances. After hearing a successful performance,
listeners may have their attitudes improved by one reaction category.

- Hostile → Unfriendly
- Unfriendly → Neutral
- Neutral → Friendly
- Friendly → Helpful

and be willing to listen.

**Counter Song**
: A bard can use music or performance to oppose certain magical effects that rely
on sound or charm. The bard must continue performing while maintaining the effect.

**Inspire Courage**
: A bard can inspire allies through music, poetry, speeches, or songs. After at
least one full round of performance, allies gain one of the following benefits:

- +1 attack rolls
- +1 saving throws
- +2 morale

The effect lasts while the performance continues and for a short period
thereafter at the DM's discretion.

**Use Scrolls**
: As the bard gains levels and magical knowledge, the bard becomes increasingly
capable of understanding and employing magical writings. Bards can use wizard
scrolls according to the normal rules governing arcane spellcasting.
